FAQ

Good questions.

Do I need to know Kubernetes?

Basic terminal comfort helps. Every mission explains what to do and why, and Launchpad tells you what failed and how to fix it. Kubernetes regulars can race for the podium.

Do I need a Civo account?

Not to register. Before race day, create a free account at civo.com/signup so your cluster — and the $250 credit — are yours to keep.

What do I need to install?

Nothing. The Launchpad Shell gives you a terminal in your browser. If you prefer your own terminal, bring the civo CLI, kubectl and git.

Can I bring a colleague?

Yes — each person registers on the form with their own email so everyone gets a seat on the grid.
