How the race works

Six missions. One clock. Every check is real.

Each mission is verified live on your cluster by Civo Launchpad — no screenshots, no honour system. Clear them in order; the fastest builders take the podium.

M0

Launch

Create a 3-node cluster in the Civo dashboard and connect it to Launchpad.

M1

Deploy & Expose

Ship NusaMart and open the shop on a public load balancer.

M2

Right-size

Fix requests and limits so pods stop being throttled and killed.

M3

Scale

Let the HPA add pods by itself when the k6 rush hits.

M4

Stay Available

Drain a node mid-sale without dropping a single customer.

FINAL

Flash Sale Ready

Warm capacity on three nodes, then survive the live burst test.

🧠 Forget passive learning

Every step changes something on a live cluster, and Launchpad tells you exactly what passed and what to fix next.

💻 Just bring a laptop

Use the Launchpad Shell — a Linux terminal in your browser with kubectl ready. Same on Windows, Mac and Linux.

FAQ

Good questions.

Do I need to know Kubernetes?

Basic terminal comfort helps. Every mission explains what to do and why, and Launchpad tells you what failed and how to fix it. Kubernetes regulars can race for the podium.

Do I need a Civo account?

Not to register. After you keep your seat we'll ask you to create a free Civo account before race day, so your cluster — and the $250 credit — are yours to keep.

What do I need to install?

Nothing. The Launchpad Shell gives you a terminal in your browser. If you prefer your own terminal, bring the civo CLI, kubectl and git.

Can I bring a colleague?

Yes — each person registers with their own email so everyone gets a seat on the grid.
