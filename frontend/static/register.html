FAQ

Good questions.

Do I need to know Kubernetes?

Basic terminal comfort helps. Every mission explains what to do and why, and Launchpad tells you what failed and how to fix it. Kubernetes regulars can race for the podium.

Why my own Civo account?

You keep what you build — and the $250 credit — after the workshop. Your cluster runs in Civo's Jakarta region; you can delete it with one click when you're done.

What do I need to install?

Nothing. The Launchpad Shell gives you a terminal in your browser. If you prefer your own terminal, bring the civo CLI, kubectl and git (on Windows: PowerShell 7 or Git Bash).

My Civo signup is stuck in review — can I still join?

Yes. Come anyway: facilitators have backup seats with a ready cluster.
